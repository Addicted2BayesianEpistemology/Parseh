Sync with Anki

Bring the edits you made inside Anki home before you export again — so re-importing adds your new cards without overwriting anything you changed there. ‹ back to the videos   ‹ the hub

1

Export your deck from Anki

In Anki: File → Export…

  • Export format: Anki Deck Package (.apkg)
  • Include: the deck you study these cards in (or All Decks — extra decks are simply ignored)
  • Include media — tick it if you have cards with screenshots or recordings, otherwise it does not matter
  • Scheduling makes no difference either way; it is never read

Save it anywhere — your Desktop is fine. The next step puts it where it belongs.

Or a package built by __APP__ — the .apkg the build button makes, from your other computer or from someone else who made the cards with __APP__: drop it in the same way. It cannot be synced (it says nothing about what Anki holds), but it is brought in: a deck you do not have yet is created, a deck you already have is given the cards it lacks, and either is then yours to build and import into Anki.

5

Rebuild and import back

Now the store and Anki agree, so this import only adds what is new here and disturbs nothing you edited there.

  • In Anki: File → Import… and pick the file you just downloaded
  • Notes are matched by their identifier, so existing cards are updated in place — your review history and due dates are kept

That is the whole loop. Do it in this order every time — sync first, export second — and the two can never fight.
