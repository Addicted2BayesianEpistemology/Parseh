The Videos door is the reading editions' method applied to a video. The
video plays at the top of the page and its whole transcript runs underneath
it, cut into small phrases. Point at a phrase and a cloud opens with what a
reading edition would print beside it: the transliteration (and, for
Japanese, the kana above it), the vocabulary, the meaning. The line being
spoken is lit as the video plays, and a click on any line plays it again
from its beginning — as many times as you like, which is the whole point.

It works for every language Parseh teaches — Persian and Arabic right to
left, Italian, French, German, Spanish, Turkish and English in Latin
letters, Hindi in Devanagari, Japanese and Chinese with the reading set over
each word — and the meanings are written in whatever language the video
says they are: English unless it says otherwise, Italian for an Italian
learning English, Persian for a Persian learning Japanese.

A video is either **on YouTube**, and streams from there, or **a film on
this machine**, which needs no internet at all. Either way the transcript,
the glosses and everything you write are files in Parseh's own folders,
read afresh on every visit: nothing is built, and nothing has to be
restarted.

## Where it is

Open the hub and press **Videos**, or go straight to
`https://localhost:8765/youtube/`. The hub's door counts the videos and
the channels, and the language chips at the top of the hub narrow the
count to one language. In **Mobile** mode the door opens the same pages:
the videos have no mobile version of their own yet.

## The short version

1. **Find a video**: pick a channel on the videos page, then a video.
2. **Watch and read**: hover a phrase for its gloss; click a line to hear
   it again; turn on **hover ⏸** to have the video wait while you read.
3. **Add a video**: the **＋ Add a video** card asks where the video is and
   who writes the glosses — an LLM whose answer the page checks, or you,
   in the player.
4. **Write it**: the ✎ in any gloss cloud opens the phrase's fields;
   **the timings** moves where a caption starts; a **+** between two lines
   holds a note of your own.
5. **Take it away**: the ⤓ in the player's bar downloads the video as one
   zip that another Parseh installs whole.
