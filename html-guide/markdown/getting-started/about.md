description: Version, author, launch-script locations, installation folder, a few friendly numbers about your shelves, and help links.
---

Open **Settings → About** to see Parseh's version, author, operating system,
Python version and installation folder. It reports the complete path of
`serve.sh` on the computer running Parseh, along with `serve.bat` for Windows
and `serve.py`. Linux and macOS use `serve.sh`; Windows uses `serve.bat`.
These are host paths, including when you view Settings from another device.

Under them, **Your time with Parseh** adds a few rough numbers, for the pleasure
of it: how many cards you have answered, how many videos and books are on your
shelves (and about how many hours of video), how many decks of exercises you
have made, and since when Parseh has been here. Nothing is recorded to make
them: they are counted from what is on the computer when the page opens, so a
deck you deleted took its answers with it, and the hours are the videos'
lengths, not the time you spent watching them.

About also states Parseh's GNU GPL licence and links to the guide, licences,
updates, the author's website and GitHub.
